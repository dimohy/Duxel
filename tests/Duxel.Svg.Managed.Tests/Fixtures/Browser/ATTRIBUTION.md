# SVG browser comparison artwork

Original SVG bytes are unmodified. Browser PNG/RGBA files are raster captures of those originals on white, produced with Chromium 156 and fixed capture conditions in `references.json`. These images are test/reference artwork, not Duxel logos. Their licenses are independent of the source code license.

The red work derives from Yellow Gallardo. Retain the attribution and share-alike terms when distributing its SVG, reference captures or adapted artwork. No source artwork was simplified or rewritten to fit the renderer. Differences produced by Duxel are recorded separately.

`Coverage/` contains original test shapes and independent Chromium pixel captures under the repository source license. They are separate from the two external artworks. Its manifest records input/output hashes and the observed GPU browser conditions; these small examples do not certify complete artwork equality.
